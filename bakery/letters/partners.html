A MESSAGE FROM OUR FOUNDER

To Our Investors & Partners

Dear investors and partners,

Every morning, long before the sun rises over Evaton, our ovens are already warm. That quiet hour is my favourite part of the day: flour on the counter, butter softening, and the first trays going in. It is when I remember why I started this bakery: to make something that brings people together.

We are building more than a bakery. We are building a local brand rooted in quality, consistency and community, one that will create jobs, support local suppliers and prove that something truly special can be made right here in our neighbourhood.

Our customers keep coming back, and we are ready for the next chapter. I invite you to grow with us. If you would like to talk about partnering with Bulelwa's Bakery & Confectionery, please reach out using the details below. I would be glad to sit down with you.

Whether you came for a cupcake or a conversation about the future, you are welcome at our table.

With gratitude and excitement for what lies ahead,

Reabetswe Dube

Founder, Bulelwa's Bakery & Confectionery

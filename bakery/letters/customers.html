A MESSAGE FROM OUR FOUNDER

To Our Customers

Dear friends and customers,

Every morning, long before the sun rises over Evaton, our ovens are already warm. That quiet hour is my favourite part of the day: flour on the counter, butter softening, and the first trays going in. It is when I remember why I started this bakery: to make something that brings people together.

Thank you. Every birthday cake you have trusted us with, every box of scones shared at a family gathering, every last-minute order for a celebration: you have let us into the most special moments of your lives. That is an honour I never take lightly.

We will keep baking honestly, with good ingredients and a lot of heart. Whether it is a wedding, a birthday, a memorial or a simple Sunday tea, you can count on us to make your table beautiful.

Whether you came for a cupcake or a conversation about the future, you are welcome at our table.

With love and gratitude,

Reabetswe Dube

Founder, Bulelwa's Bakery & Confectionery
